About the numbers

Values are Anycubic's own technical data, copied from each product page's Specification tab. Anycubic notes they vary with printer, post-cure and test equipment, so treat small differences as noise.

Anycubic's pages swap two labels: the figure they call "Bending Modulus" (about 15–70 MPa) is really flexural strength, and "Bending Strength" (about 350–3,300 MPa) is really flexural modulus. This matrix uses the corrected names. Where a collection listing and the product TDS disagreed (Water-Wash ABS-Like 3.0 hardness and elongation, BJD tensile, ABS-Like Pro 2 tensile), the product TDS value is shown.

Only ABS-Like Resin Pro 2 publishes a heat deflection temperature (60–65 °C). High Clear Resin's 92% light transmittance comes from its feature copy. Prices are the 1 kg US-store price on Oct 2, 2026; "bulk $/kg" is the promo rate for multi-kg orders. Both change often.

Source: Anycubic resin collection and the individual product pages.
